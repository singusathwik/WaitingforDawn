## 0.8.0 additions

The project is now titled Waiting for Dawn. `assets/models/mourning_ghost.glb` adds original skinned, pleated mourning veil and ragged skirt geometry to the credited Rosswet Mobile Thin Zombie base. Original character credit and CC BY 3.0 license still apply. Cloth uses the existing Poly Haven fabric texture. Rebuild with Blender 4.3.2 and `scripts/build_mourning_ghost.py`; existing five skeletal clips are preserved. The corpse retains the original rig at corrected human scale.

`assets/ui/title.png` and `assets/ui/parchment.png` were generated using the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool, text-to-image, on 2026-10-18. Higgsfield generation was attempted but rejected because the account required a paid plan; no Higgsfield asset was produced. These images are title/interface art, not a claim that the runtime map matches every architectural detail of the illustration. Original vector app icon and action symbols are code-authored SVGs. Runtime portrait pictures reuse the generated manor illustration.

Title prompt: "Create a wide 16:9 cinematic photoreal horror game title background, no text. Original fictional decaying Victorian manor with weathered wood and stone in thick blue dawn fog, a thin warm amber sunrise along horizon, frightening gaunt grieving female specter in upstairs window, abandoned vintage car by garage, three small young adult student explorers with backpacks in foreground. Large dark central negative space for UI menu. Restrained chiaroscuro, high detail film art, no gore. Save the image for use as videogame artwork."

Paper prompt: "A flat scanned antique handwritten letter background texture for a horror videogame, landscape 16:10 aspect ratio. Entire image is a single sheet of cream aged paper, extremely realistic fibrous rag paper, softly worn uneven darker edges, subtle folds and faint water stains. A few natural dark dried blood fingerprints only along the lower right margin, no bright red puddles no cartoon shapes no gore. Center 80 percent must be clean pale blank parchment with absolutely no text or marks so readable game text can be overlaid. Orthographic scan, no table, no objects, no perspective."

The updated 34.59-second introduction is original story text synthesized offline with Windows System.Speech. Wails, breathing and other effects remain original synthesis, not actor recordings. Additional furniture instances use the already credited Poly Haven models. `paper_art.gd` is retained legacy source and is no longer the journal background.

## Unreal 0.9.0

The Unreal house uses the original credited furniture and surfaces above. `unreal/tools/prepare_assets.py` exports the repair/decay furniture, hollow joinery, and animated Eleanor to FBX. It removes the source rig's unused Icosphere display mesh. `prepare_nursery_props.py` extracts the actual cradle, scanned armchair and posed keeper from the approved nursery Blender source. Unreal materials explicitly restore the original skin/fabric/oak textures; no screenshots of Blender are substituted for game geometry.

Visible co-op bodies use Epic's UE 5.7 template Manny mesh, skeleton, materials, idle/walk/jog/death animations and their rig dependencies under `unreal/WaitingForDawn/Content/Characters`. These are recognizable template mannequins, not commissioned student character art. Source: the installed `Templates/TemplateResources/High/Characters/Content` directory. Epic owns these example assets; they are not covered by any project-specific permissive license. The [Unreal Engine EULA, sections 1 and 5(b)](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal) identifies Templates as Examples and permits distributing Examples in source or object form. The Unreal runtime remains subject to Epic's EULA.

No models, audio, code, or textures were extracted from Eyes, Amnesia or the commercial reference screenshots. Narration and effects retain the original synthesized-audio provenance above.
