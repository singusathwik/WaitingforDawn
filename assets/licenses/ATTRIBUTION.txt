Adaptations: Blender legacy material converted to PBR using the original 1024px texture; five clips selected and constraints baked into glTF; model scaled and tinted cool/desaturated in-game as the house's spectral antagonist. ## Original project art



Estate layout/collision, window casing and sill meshes, pleated curtain topology, lighting, UI, icon and synthesized sound assets are original project work. Window/curtain meshes are generated by `scripts/build_architecture.py` and exported as `assets/models/architecture.glb`; their PBR materials use the credited Poly Haven maps. The old procedural face/shroud shaders are legacy source, not the active character.



Godot and its bundled dependencies retain their licenses, available in the in-game credits via `Engine.get_license_text()` / `get_license_info()`.
