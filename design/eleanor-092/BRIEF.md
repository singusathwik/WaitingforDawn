# Eleanor — mourning apparition, 0.9.2

Replace the bald, muscular silhouette with a gaunt mother in a deteriorated nightdress. Dense dark hair frames the face and falls over the shoulders. Thin arms end in tapered nails; layered cloth, a frayed hem and a small keepsake pendant supply readable detail at corridor distance.

Use Blender geometry and baked textures, preserving the credited Rosswet armature and five existing clips. Keep the original sources unchanged. Target fewer than 35,000 triangles, no hair simulation, no alpha-blended hair, and 1024-pixel material maps for Android. The facial source texture uses the built-in image generator; no stock or third-party model was downloaded. Preserve the working capsule, master-root scale, chase logic and multiplayer behavior.

Deliver editable packed Blender source, FBX, material maps, provenance/mesh inspection, Blender renders and actual Unreal/Android captures.
